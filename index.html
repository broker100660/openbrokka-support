Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a property inspection?

Open a property listing in the app, tap "Book Inspection," choose a time, and complete payment to confirm your slot.

What payment methods are supported?

OpenBrokkA supports Mobile Money (Airtel Money, MTN) and card payments via Pesapal.

How do I upload a property listing?

Property uploads are done through the OpenBrokkA Broker app, available to verified brokers. If you're a broker without access, contact support to get set up.

I didn't receive a payment confirmation. What should I do?

Check your Bookings tab first. If the payment doesn't reflect within a few minutes, email us with your transaction reference and we'll look into it.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email support@openbrokka.com with the request and we'll process it within a few business days.

How do I report an issue with a listing or a broker?

Use the "Customer Care" button in the app to call us directly or write us a message.
